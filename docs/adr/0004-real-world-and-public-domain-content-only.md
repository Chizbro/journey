# The catalogue carries only real-world and public-domain routes

Journeys are drawn from real trails (Camino de Santiago, Bibbulmun Track, Hadrian's Wall,
Appalachian Trail, Kumano Kodo) and out-of-copyright fiction (The Odyssey, Around the World in
Eighty Days, The Pilgrim's Progress). No in-copyright fiction, however obvious a fit.

The obvious flagship Journey is Frodo's walk from the Shire to Mount Doom, and we are not
shipping it. Routes and distances are not copyrightable and neither are real place names, but
"Rivendell", "Lothlórien" and "Frodo" are protected, and the Tolkien Estate and Middle-earth
Enterprises enforce aggressively — including against small apps. A takedown would arrive exactly
when the app became popular enough to be noticed.

Renaming it — "The Ring-bearer's Road", with unnamed Landmarks — does not remove the risk and
does remove the appeal.

## Consequences

- This is a content constraint, not a product one. A Journey is a distance plus ordered Landmarks
  (ADR-0002), so The Odyssey drops into the model exactly as well as Mordor would have.
- Licensing a marquee IP properly remains open once there are users worth licensing to.
- Do not add in-copyright routes to the catalogue as a convenience. That is the failure mode this
  ADR exists to prevent.
